Scope and Limitations

revenue-kun outputs income-estimation values (収益試算値) in an Excel workbook. It does not provide a real-estate appraisal, appraisal value, investment recommendation, legal opinion, or tax opinion. It does not decide cap rates, vacancy assumptions, operating expense assumptions, legal treatment, tax treatment, or investment suitability. Users are responsible for entering judgment-based assumptions and reviewing all extraction results, formulas, assumptions, and outputs.

Unsupported Inputs

OCR, scanned PDFs, smartphone-captured images, and hosted SaaS uploads are outside the supported scope. revenue-kun supports CSV files and text-based PDFs only.
